# 🌍 Free AI Tools & Developer Resources

A curated global directory of free, open-source, and free-tier tools for students, developers, AI builders, hackathon teams, and startups.

> **Important:** Free plans and quotas change. Always verify the provider's official pricing page before using a service in production. Never assume a tool is unlimited or permanently free.

## 🏷️ Free-status labels

- 🟢 **Open source / self-hosted:** Software is available to run yourself; infrastructure may cost money.
- 🔵 **Free tier:** Free usage is available with limits such as requests, storage, bandwidth, or compute.
- 🟡 **Free credits / eligibility:** Free access may expire or require student, startup, or account eligibility.
- 🔴 **Paid after allowance:** Payment may be required after the free quota or credits are consumed.

## 🤝 Contribution Guidelines

1. Verify the tool's official website and pricing page.
2. Add the exact free quota when it is publicly documented.
3. Mention billing requirements, expiration, and important restrictions.
4. Avoid claims such as **unlimited free** unless officially guaranteed.
5. Include a **Last verified** date for changing quotas.
6. Keep links official and avoid referral links.

## 📌 Disclaimer

This repository is a community-oriented resource list, not a guarantee of free access. Providers can change pricing, quotas, eligibility, licensing, and availability at any time. Always check official documentation before relying on a tool for a project.
